## Why there is no number here

A rate is a promise with a date attached, and a promise needs a source. The project documents the programme's existence; it does not publish a rate, a minimum balance, a lock period or a crediting schedule in the material available. Publishing an estimate would be worse than publishing nothing, because a reader would plan around a figure that no one agreed to.

This is also why a third-party calculator cannot answer the question. A calculator needs inputs the project has not published, so any output is its own assumption dressed as your return.

## What a fixed-term programme decides

Four decisions matter, and each one changes the outcome more than the headline rate does:

- **Which assets qualify.** Eligibility is separate from the list of supported networks.
- **The term.** A fixed term means the balance is committed for a period, and early movement can end eligibility.
- **Crediting.** Rewards can be paid continuously, at the end of a term, or on a schedule.
- **What happens on movement.** Sending part of a qualifying balance mid-term may cancel or reduce it.

When you read terms, look for those four before the percentage. A high rate with an unclear term is a worse deal than a modest one with clear rules.

## What to ask support

Ask four questions and keep the answers: does this specific asset qualify, what is the term, when are rewards credited, and what cancels eligibility. Ask for the current term sheet rather than a summary. If the answer is "check the website", ask which page, and note the date you asked.

That record is what makes a later dispute resolvable. It also stops the loop where a reader asks the same question in three channels and gets three summaries.

The mechanism behind the programme is covered separately in <a href="https://github.com/vidulum-app/vidulum-wallet-staking/blob/main/guides/vidulum-staking-rewards-vdl-programme.md">Vidulum VDL staking rewards programme</a>.

## Common claims that are not terms

- A rate quoted in a video with no date and no source.
- A calculator that asks you to enter an amount and returns a projection.
- A number repeated in a chat group, sourced to "the team said".
- An old figure from a previous term, still circulating.

None of these are terms. Terms have an owner, a date and a scope.

Custody is covered in <a href="https://github.com/vidulum-app/vidulum-wallet-help/blob/main/guides/is-vidulum-wallet-safe-custody-and-keys.md">is the Vidulum Wallet safe to hold keys</a> before you commit coins to a term.

## FAQ

### What is the current VDL interest rate?

No rate is published in the material behind this page, so this page does not state one. The project's own terms are the only source.

### Why do calculators show a number?

Because they assume one. A projection is not a published term, and it is not a commitment by anyone.

### Can the rate change?

Terms of this kind are set by the project and can change between periods. Read the current sheet each time you commit.

### Does a higher rate mean a better deal?

Not on its own. Term, crediting schedule and early-exit rules usually matter more than the headline figure.
